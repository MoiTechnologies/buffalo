Buffalo Privacy Policy 1. Introduction & Statutory Framework Buffalo ("Ride with ease") is operated by MoiTech Business Group ("MoiTech", "Buffalo", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, share, and protect your personal data in strict compliance with the **Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019** and the **Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021** of the Republic of Uganda. By registering, accessing, or using Buffalo, you provide explicit consent to the data collection and processing practices described herein. 2. Policy Versioning & Updates MoiTech reserves the right to revise this Privacy Policy at any time. When material changes occur, we will notify you by incrementing the Policy Version number in system settings, issuing in-app alerts, or displaying a mandatory policy review modal. Your continued use of the Platform after such updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy. 3. Personal Data We Collect A. Information Provided Directly by You - Account Identity: Full name, phone number, email address, gender, age, and profile photos. - Verification & Compliance Documents: Government-issued ID cards, driving licenses, vehicle logs, and 3-second video selfies (for Buffalo Connect and Driver verification). - Financial & Wallet Ledgers: Mobile Money account phone numbers (MTN and Airtel Uganda), deposit/withdrawal transaction logs, wallet balances, and Investment Club Vault share records. We store secure transaction reference codes; we NEVER store Mobile Money PINs or full debit/credit card details. - Logistics & Concierge Order Details: Sender and recipient names, phone numbers, delivery address coordinates, and package verification photos. - Buffalo Connect Profile Content: Bio text, age, gender, interests, and profile photo galleries. B. Automatic Technical & Location Collection - Precise Real-time Location Data: - Riders & Members: Collected during active app usage to calculate micro-proximity distance indicators (e.g. `⚡ Super Close <200m`), display nearby member feeds, and match rides/orders. - Drivers & Providers: Precise real-time and background location is collected even when the app is closed. This is mandatory for safety monitoring, fare verification, trip routing, and Admin dispatch controls. - Analytics & Maps: Integrated Google Maps APIs collect mapping telemetry governed by Google's Privacy Policy. 4. Purposes of Data Processing - Service Provisioning: To facilitate rides, deliveries, concierge orders, wallet withdrawals, and Investment Vault yield distributions. - Micro-Proximity & Matching: To rank nearby member feeds and calculate proximity distance badges in Buffalo Connect. - Safety, Fraud & Identity Review: To verify driver documents, perform side-by-side verification of selfie videos against profile photos, prevent financial fraud, and monitor real-time safety. - Legal & Tax Compliance: To maintain mandatory audit records in compliance with Ugandan financial, transport, and tax laws. 5. Domain-Specific Data Processing Disclosures A. Delivery & Concierge Agency Model - ORDER DATA DISCLOSURE: For non-partnered merchants, Buffalo operates under a Personal Shopper / Concierge Agency model. Necessary item names and food preferences are shared with the assigned Driver (acting as your Direct Agent) to complete the purchase at the merchant point of sale on your behalf. - STORE CLAIM REQUEST LEDGER & NON-OWNERSHIP ACKNOWLEDGMENT: When a user or partner submits a claim request for an unassigned restaurant or store listing, we collect the user's full name, phone number ID, age confirmation declaration, non-ownership acknowledgment, and timestamp to verify local courier fulfillment eligibility, prevent fraudulent claims, and enforce delivery compliance. B. Buffalo Connect (Social Networking, Identity Verification & Chat) - PROFILE VISIBILITY & PROXIMITY DISPLAY: Creating a Buffalo Connect profile displays your name, age, gender, bio, profile photo gallery, verification badge, and micro-proximity distance badge (`⚡ Super Close <200m`) to nearby members. - IDENTITY VIDEO SELFIE PROCESSING: Submitted 3-second video selfies and thumbnail frames are stored in secure Firebase Storage (`connect_verifications/`) and accessed exclusively by authorized Admins to compare against profile photos for blue badge verification. - CHAT ENCRYPTION & UNMATCH EXCLUSION LEDGER: Direct chat messages are encrypted during transit and stored securely. Unmatching a member immediately deletes the active chat history and logs an unmatch record (`unmatchedUntil`) to exclude that member from appearing in your feed for 180 days (6 months). - HIDDEN MODE PRIVACY: Toggling profile visibility OFF immediately pauses location broadcasting and hides your profile from the nearby feed. C. Universal Wallet, Inflow Controls & Phone Number Migrations - AUTHORIZED INFLOW CHANNELS: Funds can ONLY enter a user's wallet via verified Mobile Money deposits, authorized Agent cash deposits, or P2P transfers. Artificial admin bonuses are eliminated. - ACCOUNT FREEZING: Wallet and Vault activities may be frozen for security or legal review. Transacting is paused during freeze while Support remains accessible. - MOBILE MONEY WITHDRAWALS: Withdrawal requests to Mobile Money (MTN or Airtel Uganda) process your phone number ID and withdrawal amount through verified Mobile Money gateway channels. - PHONE NUMBER MIGRATION TRANSFERS: When requesting a phone number change, upon verified OTP confirmation, all wallet balances, audit history, Investment Vault capital, yield records, notifications, and profile data transfer seamlessly from your old phone number ID to your new phone number ID. Your former number ID is deactivated to prevent duplicate access. - IMMUTABLE TRANSACTION AUDIT LEDGER: All wallet deposits, withdrawals, P2P transfers, migration fees, and interest distributions are recorded permanently in your account's digital audit ledger. D. Buffalo Investment Club & Capital Vault (Private Small Partnership) - MUTUAL PRIVATE PARTNERSHIP LEDGER: Capital committed to the Investment Club Vault operates on mutual grounds as a private small partnership request accepted by MoiTech Business Group, representing small liquidated shares. Vault records, weekly interest earnings, duration tenures (28-day 4-week month structures), auto-renew preferences (OFF by default), automated server-side maturity unlocks, Small Claims Procedure (SCP) logs, administrative contract termination records, and contract nullification timestamps are processed automatically by server-side engines and logged transparently in your audit ledger. 6. Data Sharing & Disclosure - SERVICE PROVIDERS: Necessary trip and order details are shared with assigned Drivers to fulfill requests. - LAW ENFORCEMENT & LEGAL PROCESS: Data is disclosed when mandated by Ugandan courts, law enforcement warrants, or to investigate severe safety breaches or financial fraud. - NO DATA SELLING: MoiTech NEVER sells, rents, or monetizes your personal data to third-party advertisers. 7. Data Retention & Mandatory Audits - Financial & Transaction Ledgers: Retained for **7 years** pursuant to Ugandan tax and financial data regulations. - Location & Safety Logs: Retained for **12 months** for safety audit and dispute resolution purposes. - Unmatch & Block Records: Retained for **180 days (6 months)** to enforce feed exclusion. 8. User Rights & Account Deletion Process - Account Deletion Workflow: 1. Upon submitting an account deletion request via Support, your account is immediately deactivated and hidden from public view. 2. Your phone number ID is timestamped to enter a mandatory **30-day Cooling-off & Audit Period** to verify that no pending wallet disputes, unpaid order balances, safety reports, or active police investigations exist. 3. Final permanent erasure of personal data occurs after 30 days, subject to 7-year statutory financial retention obligations. - Wallet Balance Cashout: You retain the right to withdraw your remaining wallet balance prior to account closure via the Universal Wallet Withdrawal interface or Support Chat. 9. Data Security We employ industry-standard security measures, including TLS 1.3 transport encryption, AES-256 database storage encryption, and strict role-based admin access controls. 10.
