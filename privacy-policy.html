Buffalo Privacy Policy 1. Introduction Buffalo ("Ride with ease") is operated by MoiTech. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data in accordance with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda. By using Buffalo, you consent to the practices described in this policy. 2. Information We Collect A. Information You Provide - Account Information: Name, phone number, email, and profile photo. - Identity Verification: Government-issued ID, driver licenses, and vehicle documentation (for providers). - Financial Data: Transaction history related to the Buffalo Wallet, including top-up references and payment methods. - Logistics Data: For Buffalo Delivery, we collect sender/recipient names, phone numbers, and photos of packages. B. Automatic Collection - Location Data: We collect precise location data. - For Riders: To match you with nearby providers and track active trips. - For Drivers/Providers: We collect and monitor real-time location and "last known" location even when the app is in the background. THIS DATA IS ACCESSIBLE BY BUFFALO ADMINS for security monitoring, service optimization, and verified distance tracking. - Device & Usage Data: IP address, device type, and app interaction logs. 3. How We Use Your Information - To provide and maintain the Services (Rides, Orders, Deliveries). - To manage your Buffalo Wallet and process payments. - For safety and security: This includes real-time Admin monitoring of Driver locations to ensure passenger safety and assist in emergency response. - To comply with legal obligations under Ugandan law. 4. Data Sharing We share your data with: - Third-Party Providers (Drivers/Merchants) to fulfill your requests. - Legal Authorities when required by the Data Protection and Privacy Act or for safety investigations. - We DO NOT sell your personal data to third parties. 5. Your Rights & Account Deletion Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you have the right to: - Access your data and request a copy. - Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data. - Object to or restrict the processing of your data. - Request deletion of your data: - When you initiate account deletion, your account is immediately deactivated and removed from public view. - Your User ID is renamed to include the date and time of the request to prevent re-registration with the same identity during the cooling-off period. - Data is retained in a "Scheduled for Deletion" state for 30 days to allow for dispute resolution or legal compliance. - Permanent deletion of all personal data, history, and files occurs after 30 days and requires manual Admin Approval to ensure no pending liabilities or safety investigations exist. - Lodge a complaint with the National Information Technology Authority (NITA-U). 6. Data Retention We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services and comply with legal requirements. - Active data: Retained while account is active. - Deletion queue: Retained for 30 days after a deletion request. - Driver location logs: Kept for a minimum of 12 months for security audits. - Transaction records: Typically held for a minimum of 7 years as per Ugandan financial regulations. 7. Security We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction. 8.
